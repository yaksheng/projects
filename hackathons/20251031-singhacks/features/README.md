# Feature Scope

## Conversational Discovery

The interface gathers travel details, identifies the user's questions, and explains coverage in plain language.

## Multimodal Intake

The workflow accepts text, voice, images, and documents. Speech and document services convert those inputs into structured information for review.

## Policy Workflow

The application organizes stated needs and presents relevant coverage options.

## Claim Intake

Users provide incident details and supporting documents through conversation. The application checks for missing information before claim submission.

## Payments and Status

The project includes payment confirmation and workflow status interactions.

## Human Review

Complex, regulated, or ambiguous questions are routed to an authorized person rather than resolved automatically.
