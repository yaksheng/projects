# Demo Flow

The demonstration follows a traveler from policy discovery through claim intake.

## Sequence

1. Introduce the travel insurance problem.
2. Collect destination, duration, and coverage needs through conversation.
3. Explain relevant coverage options in plain language.
4. Walk through a claim intake using supporting documents.
5. Show where insurer, identity, and payment services enter the workflow.
6. Explain which decisions require an authorized provider or human review.

## Demonstration Boundaries

- Policy issuance and payment are simulated.
- Claim review and settlement are simulated.
- Product terms, prices, and eligibility depend on an authorized insurer.
