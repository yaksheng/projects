# Bilingual Support

SourceVoice's bilingual capabilities break down language barriers in international injection molding negotiations, enabling seamless communication between English and Mandarin speakers.

## Core Capabilities

### Real-time Translation
- **Bidirectional translation** between English and Mandarin during conversations
- **Context-aware translation** that preserves industry-specific terminology
- **Natural language understanding** for idiomatic expressions
- **Dynamic language detection** that automatically identifies user language

### Industry-Specific Terminology
- **Manufacturing glossary** with over 1,000 injection molding terms
- **Contextual term mapping** to ensure accurate translation
- **Consistent terminology** across all interactions
- **Custom term management** for specialized vocabulary

### Cultural Adaptation
- **Negotiation style adaptation** based on cultural context
- **Formal/informal tone adjustment** for appropriate communication
- **Business etiquette guidance** for cross-cultural interactions
- **Localized examples** relevant to regional manufacturing practices

The bilingual system is designed to be:
