# Bilingual Support

SourceVoice supports English and Mandarin interaction paths for text, speech recognition, model responses, and text-to-speech output.

## Prototype Behavior

- Select or detect the conversation language.
- Transcribe supported browser speech input.
- Ask the model to preserve injection-molding terminology.
- Generate text and optional speech output in the selected language.
- Maintain the language choice in client state.

## Limitations

Translation quality, terminology consistency, language detection, accents, and speech recognition were not formally evaluated during the four-hour build. Generated wording should be reviewed by a bilingual specialist before supplier communication, contract use, or a consequential decision.

## Future Work

- Build a reviewed glossary from documented manufacturing sources.
- Evaluate representative technical and negotiation scenarios.
- Let users inspect and correct source and translated text side by side.
- Record uncertainty and preserve the original language in exports.
